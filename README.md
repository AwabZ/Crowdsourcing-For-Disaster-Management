## 🛰️ Leveraging Crowdsourcing: Mobile Application for Disaster Management
This project, originally drafted in Fall 2025, explores the implementation of lightweight mobile applications to accelerate disaster response through public contribution. Please be aware that this is a very old version that will be heavily updated, enhanced, and improved in future iterations to meet current frontier standards.

## 💡 Core Concept (Legacy Version)
The primary goal was to decentralize disaster management by moving away from strictly centralized authorities and leveraging the collective intelligence of the public.  

- Crowdsourcing: General information gathering from the public.  

- Crowdtasking: Assigning specific, verified tasks to volunteers to increase operational efficiency.

## 🛠️ Legacy Feature Set
The original design focused on several key modules that are now considered outdated and will be heavily updated, enhanced, and improved:

1. Data Collection & Processing
- Image-Based Submission: Users submit photos of hazards.  

- Metadata Extraction: Extracting GPS and time data from photos to verify incident locations.  

- Trust Score System: A legacy method for weighting volunteer reports based on past accuracy.  

2. Social Media Extraction Algorithm
- Web Crawlers: Using APIs (e.g., legacy Twitter API) to search for keywords like "flood" or "earthquake".  

- Computer Vision (Legacy): Basic utilization of OpenCV or Google Vision API to detect stock images or AI-generated misinformation.  

3. Navigation & Offline Support
- Modified Dijkstra’s Algorithm: A risk-weighted shortest-path algorithm that assigns 60% weight to distance and 40% to the hazard risk score.  

- Mesh Networking (P2P): Utilizing Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Wi-Fi Direct to share hazard alerts device-to-device when internet is unavailable.  

- Cached Maps: Pre-downloaded evacuation routes that show the "last updated" timestamp.

## 🎮 Gamification & Motivation
To keep volunteers engaged, the very old version of the app included basic gamification:  

- Points System: Earning "+50 pts" for hazard photos or "+10 pts" for verifying reports.  

- Redeemable Badges: Showcasing contributions to the community.
